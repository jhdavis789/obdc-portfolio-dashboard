C. COLLATERAL ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA, ADVANCE RATES, CONCENTRATION LIMITS
================================================================================

The 10-K provides GENERAL descriptions (not specific advance rate percentages) for each facility:

REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY:
- Borrowing base applies "different advance rates to different types of assets (based on their
  value as determined pursuant to the Revolving Credit Facility)"
- Covenants include: (a) limitations on additional indebtedness and liens, (b) limitations on
  certain investments, (c) limitations on restricted payments, (d) minimum stockholders' equity,
  (e) ratio of total assets (less non-debt liabilities) to total indebtedness >= 1.5:1.0,
  (f) limitations on pledging unencumbered assets, (g) limitations on agreements prohibiting liens
- Available borrowing subject to borrowing base and other restrictions

REVOLVING FUNDING FACILITY (Ares Capital CP):
- Borrowing base applies "different advance rates to different types of assets held by Ares Capital CP"
- Restrictions: sector concentrations, loan size, payment frequency and status, collateral interests,
  loans with fixed rates, portfolio company leverage
- All affect borrowing base and therefore available borrowing

SMBC FUNDING FACILITY (ACJB):
- Borrowing base applies "an advance rate to assets held by ACJB"
- Same restriction categories as Revolving Funding Facility

BNP FUNDING FACILITY (AFB):
- Borrowing base applies "an advance rate to assets held by AFB"
- Same restriction categories as Revolving Funding Facility

CLO SECURITIZATIONS:
- ADL CLO 1, ADL CLO 4, ADL CLO 7 contain conditions for acquiring additional loans
  "in accordance with rating agency criteria or as otherwise agreed with certain institutional investors"
- CLOs are backed by "diversified portfolio of first lien senior secured loans"

NOTE: Specific advance rate percentages, concentration limit percentages, and detailed eligibility
criteria are NOT disclosed in the 10-K filing. These are contained in the underlying credit
agreements which are filed as exhibits but are hundreds of pages long.

D. FAIR VALUE OF ASSETS PLEDGED AS COLLATERAL
================================================================================

The 10-K does NOT provide a breakdown of fair value of assets pledged to each individual facility.

What IS disclosed:
- Total investments at fair value: $29,484.8 million (12/31/2025)
- Footnote (2) on the Schedule of Investments marks individual investments as pledged, but does
  NOT distinguish which specific facility they are pledged to
- Each sub-facility (Ares Capital CP, ACJB, AFB, ADL CLO 1/4/7) holds its own segregated pool
  of assets as collateral
- The Revolving Credit Facility is secured by "certain assets in the Company's portfolio" and
  explicitly EXCLUDES investments held by subsidiaries for other facilities

The only way to determine fair value per facility would be to:
1. Review each line item in the Schedule of Investments to determine which subsidiary holds it
   (not disclosed at the line-item level in the 10-K)
2. Or review subsidiary-level financial statements in the 10-K/A filing

APPROXIMATE AVAILABLE BORROWING CAPACITY (implied collateral adequacy):
- Revolving Credit Facility: $3,411M available (of $5,493M committed)
- Total across all credit facilities: ~$5.5 billion available for additional borrowings
- Approximately $4.5 billion was outstanding under the Credit Facilities
  (Revolving Credit Facility + Revolving Funding Facility + SMBC + BNP)
