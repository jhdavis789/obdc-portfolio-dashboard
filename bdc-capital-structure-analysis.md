# BDC Capital Structure & Asset Ringfencing Analysis

*Analysis date: March 2026 | Data as of December 31, 2025*
*Source: OBDC 10-K (FY2025), ARCC public filings, SEC EDGAR*

This document summarizes the capital structures of two large publicly traded BDCs — **Blue Owl Capital Corporation (OBDC)** and **Ares Capital Corporation (ARCC)** — and examines how their senior secured debt ringfences specific pools of portfolio assets, using actual data from SEC filings.

---

### Facility Structures (from 10-K Note 5)

**Revolving Credit Facility** (Truist Bank, as Administrative Agent):
- $4.025B total = $75M term loan + $3.95B revolving (increased from $3.83B on 11/6/2025)
- Accordion feature up to $5.5B
- Secured by a **perfected first-priority interest in substantially all portfolio investments** held by the Company and each Guarantor, subject to certain exceptions
- Borrowing base with advance rates based on portfolio asset types
- Minimum asset coverage ratio: 1.50x
- Undrawn fee: 0.35% on daily undrawn amounts
- Reduced by $42.2M of outstanding letters of credit

**SPV Asset Facility II** (ORCC Financing II LLC → Natixis as Admin Agent):
- $300M revolving commitments
- Subject to overcollateralization ratio test based on ORCC Financing II's asset values
- Must satisfy interest coverage ratio, concentration limits, collateral quality tests
- Assets secured by perfected first priority security interest in subsidiary assets
- **Assets NOT available to pay OBDC's other debts**

**SPV Asset Facility V** (ORCC III Financing LLC → Société Générale):
- $525M (decreased from $625M on 12/8/2023), multi-currency
- Subject to borrowing base determined by value and types of assets
- $100M swingline sub-limit
- Assumed from OBDE in the January 2025 merger

**SPV Asset Facility VI** (ORCC III Financing II LLC → Deutsche Bank):
- $500M (increased from $350M on 10/10/2024)
- Subject to borrowing base, interest spread, WAC tests, concentration limits, collateral quality tests
- Make-whole fee if undrawn commitments exceed certain thresholds
- Assumed from OBDE in the January 2025 merger

**SPV Asset Facility VII** (OBDC III Financing III LLC → Bank of America):
- $300M, multi-currency
- Applicable rate: 1.75%-2.50% depending on collateral composition
- Prepayment penalty if commitments terminated in first two years
- Assumed from OBDE in the January 2025 merger

**CLOs I, III, IV, V, VII, X, XIV** (Owl Rock CLO entities):
- Term debt securitizations backed by portfolios of middle-market loans
- CLO I structure: $242M Class A (AAA, S+180), $30M Class A-F (AAA, fixed 4.165%), $68M Class B (AA, S+270), $50M Class A Loans
- The Adviser (Blue Owl) serves as collateral manager; routinely waives management fees
- CLO notes issued under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act (private placement)
- **Assets pledged to CLO debt holders NOT available to pay OBDC's debts**

### What's Actually in the Ringfence? (from 10-K Schedule of Investments)

The 10-K Schedule of Investments contains two critical footnotes:
- **(25)** "Unless otherwise indicated, the Company's portfolio companies are pledged as collateral supporting the amounts outstanding under the Revolving Credit Facility, SPV Asset Facilities and CLOs."
- **(26)** "Investment is not pledged as collateral for the credit facilities."

By mapping every investment entry in the Schedule to its footnote markers:

| Pool | Positions | Fair Value | Description |
|------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Pledged** (no footnote 26) | ~61 | ~$1,770M | Collateral for the Revolving Credit Facility borrowing base |
| **Not pledged** (footnote 26) | ~302 | ~$14,500M | Either inside SPV/CLO subsidiaries (securing subsidiary debt) or truly unencumbered |
| **Total** | 365 | $16,474M | |

**Interpretation:** Footnote (26) means "not pledged to the parent company's credit facilities." This encompasses two categories:
1. Investments that have been **contributed to SPV/CLO subsidiaries** — these secure the SPV/CLO's own borrowings but are NOT available to the parent's revolving credit facility or unsecured creditors
2. Investments that are **truly unencumbered** — not pledged to any facility

### Secured Facilities

- Administrative agent: JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.

- Pricing: SOFR + 10 bps credit spread adjustment + 175-187.5 bps applicable spread

**ARCC FB Funding LLC (BNP Funding Facility):**

- Minimum **96%** Senior Secured Loans and Eligible Investments

- Maximum **17.5%** of assets rated CCC+ or below (S&P/Fitch)
- Concentration limits: ≤15% First Lien Last Out / Second Lien combined; ≤10% Second Lien alone
- Must satisfy Maximum Weighted Average Life Test

## 3. Comparative Analysis

### Side-by-Side

| Metric | OBDC | ARCC |
|--------|------|------|
| **Total assets (FV)** | $16.5B | $29.5B |
| **Total debt** | $9.4B | ~$16.0B |
| **Net debt/equity** | 1.19x | 1.08x |
| **Asset coverage** | 178% | ~185% |
| **Secured debt** | $4.4B (46.5%) | ~$6.9B (~43%) |
| **Unsecured notes** | $5.0B (53.5%) | ~$9.1B (~57%) |
| **Available liquidity** | $4.2B | >$6.0B |
| **Ringfencing vehicles** | 4 SPVs + 7 CLOs + SLF JV | AFB + other subs |
| **Investments pledged to parent revolver** | ~61 ($1.8B) | Not separately disclosed |
| **Investments NOT pledged to parent** | ~302 ($14.5B) | Not separately disclosed |

### Key Structural Differences

1. **CLO usage:** OBDC has seven active CLO transactions ($2.3B outstanding), providing term-matched, non-recourse funding. ARCC relies more on revolving subsidiary facilities.

2. **Ringfencing granularity:** OBDC's 4 SPVs + 7 CLOs + SLF JV create many discrete collateral pools. ARCC's approach uses fewer, larger ringfenced subsidiaries (primarily AFB). OBDC's structure is partly a legacy of the January 2025 OBDE merger, which brought SPV Facilities V, VI, and VII into the structure.

3. **Disclosure:** OBDC's Schedule of Investments uses footnotes (25)/(26) to mark every investment as pledged or not. ARCC discloses eligibility criteria for the BNP Facility (96% senior secured, ≤4% 2L, etc.) but the 10-K's per-investment footnoting follows a similar approach.

4. **Leverage:** ARCC runs at lower leverage (1.08x vs. 1.19x), giving it more cushion relative to the 2:1 statutory BDC leverage limit.

5. **Revolver utilization:** OBDC draws $1.0B on a $4.0B revolver (25% utilization) vs. ARCC drawing ~$3.4B on $5.5B (~62%). OBDC has relatively more "dry powder" in its revolver.

### Implications for Unsecured Creditors

The critical structural subordination question: *what's left for unsecured noteholders?*

**OBDC:** Only 61 positions ($1.8B) are pledged to the parent-level revolving credit facility. The remaining $14.5B is marked "not pledged to credit facilities" — this pool includes both:
- Assets inside SPVs/CLOs (~$5.5B est.) that secure subsidiary debt and are NOT available to unsecured creditors
- Truly unencumbered assets (~$9.2B est.) that would be available in a liquidation

Coverage on unsecured notes from unencumbered assets: ~1.8x. Including revolver collateral surplus: higher.

**ARCC:** ~$29.5B total FV minus ~$6.9B secured = ~$22.6B supporting ~$9.1B unsecured notes. Coverage: ~2.5x.

Both BDCs maintain investment-grade ratings on their unsecured debt.
